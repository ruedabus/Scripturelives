MAV:    A large, powerful male Bullmastiff dog weighing 145 lbs,
        with a broad flat head, wide-set dark eyes, a short square
        muzzle with a slight underbite, thick muscular neck and body,
        and a short dense fawn/tan coat. Despite his size he has
        a gentle, brave, expressive face. Adventurous and loyal.

MOONY:  A small 13 lb male dapple Dachshund (doxie) with the
        classic long body, very short legs, long floppy ears,
        and a dapple coat — patches of lighter and darker tan/brown/
        cream with classic dachshund merle dapple markings.
        Long narrow snout, soulful dark eyes, expressive face.
        Clever, curious, and full of personality despite his size.

JESUS:  A man with shoulder-length brown hair, a short beard, and
        kind, deeply certain eyes. Wearing a white/cream outer robe
        over a light brown tunic. Calm, warm, and authoritative.
